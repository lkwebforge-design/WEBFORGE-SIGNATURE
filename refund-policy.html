Refund Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

1. Scope

This policy applies to website design and development, SEO, maintenance and other digital services supplied by WebForge Signature. It does not apply to third-party products or subscriptions purchased directly from their providers.

2. Before work begins

If a client cancels a project before work has started, any refund of payments received will be assessed against the accepted quotation, any non-recoverable costs already incurred, and applicable law. The applicable cancellation terms should be confirmed in writing before payment.

3. After work has started

Once work has begun, payments may cover time spent, completed milestones, design or development work, and costs committed for the project. If a project is cancelled, we will review the work completed and amounts paid and determine whether any balance is refundable under the written agreement and applicable law. A refund is not automatic for work already completed or delivered.

4. Ongoing services

For recurring maintenance or SEO services, cancellation and any refund of unused service periods will follow the terms agreed at purchase. Third-party fees, domain registrations, hosting charges, software licences and other non-refundable provider charges may not be refundable by WebForge Signature.

5. Service concerns and corrections

If a delivered item does not match the agreed written scope, contact us promptly with details. We will review the issue and, where appropriate, arrange a correction or other reasonable resolution in line with the project agreement and applicable law.

6. How to request a refund

Email lkwebforge@gmail.com with your name, project or invoice reference, payment details (do not send full card numbers or security codes), and the reason for your request. We will review the request and respond using the contact details provided.

7. Statutory rights

Nothing in this policy is intended to exclude or limit rights that cannot lawfully be excluded under applicable consumer or other laws. If this policy conflicts with mandatory legal requirements, those requirements will apply.
