Delivery Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

1. Digital service delivery

WebForge Signature provides digital services. We do not ship physical products through this website. Project deliverables are normally provided electronically, such as through a live website URL, agreed file-sharing method, email, or access to the relevant website platform.

2. Delivery timelines

Estimated start dates, milestones and completion dates are agreed with the client in the written quotation or project communication. Timelines depend on project scope, receipt of required content and access, timely feedback and approvals, and any third-party services. We will communicate material delays where reasonably possible.

3. Website launch and handover

Where included in the agreed scope, delivery may include publishing the website to the agreed domain or hosting environment and handing over the agreed access details, files or instructions. Domain, hosting, email, premium software and other third-party costs are included only when expressly stated in writing.

4. SEO and maintenance services

SEO and maintenance are delivered according to the tasks, service period and reporting or update schedule agreed in writing. Search engine processing and ranking changes are controlled by third parties and are not guaranteed delivery outcomes.

5. Client cooperation

The client should provide requested content, credentials, approvals and feedback without undue delay. If these are delayed, the delivery schedule may need to be adjusted.

6. Delivery issues

If a link, file or handover item is missing or inaccessible, contact us at lkwebforge@gmail.com and include your project or invoice reference. We will review the issue and work with you to provide or restore the agreed deliverable.
